## Trust (SEC)

- **SEC-1** `done` **A client-sent GUID is a selector, never an authority.** The server decides whose items a packet may name from the session, and the GUID only picks one item within that scope. One scoped lookup in the item layer, `lookup(scope, guid)`, serves every handler; no handler runs a bare lookup by GUID on the item table. The scopes: the character's own held items (inventory, bank, cursor, their trader listings, their parcels); the current listings of the trader a Bazaar request names (buy and inspect, as Live allows); the character's own retired items (the Recovery tab). A GUID outside the scope, an all-zero or placeholder GUID (`{0, 0, n}`, what the client gives a locally split stack), or text that fails the round-trip decode is refused and logged at Warning with the character, the GUID and the scope tried; what follows is the server owner's call. Every update, move, listing, sale, destruction or recovery follows only from a scoped lookup, so one expressed against an item the character does not hold cannot be written. The paths on this branch: Bazaar buy and inspect, trader price update, evolving XP transfer, parcels, the Recovery tab. Built 2026-11-03 (`ItemLayer::Lookup(db, content_db, scope, guid, requester)` with `ItemScope::ListedBy(trader)`, the one scope with a consumer today; `HeldBy`/`RetiredOf` are added when parcels and the Recovery tab need them). The refusal is `ItemLayer::LogRefusal` (Warning, with the character, `ItemGUIDText(guid)` and `scope.Text()`); `guid.first == 0` covers the all-zero, placeholder and failed-decode cases (`ItemGUIDFromField` already yields a zero GUID for undecodable text). The Bazaar inspect (`DoBazaarInspect`), the cross-zone/parcel buy (`BuyTraderItemOutsideBazaar`) and the single-listing resend (`SendSingleTraderItem`) resolve through `Lookup`; `TraderRepository::GetListing` is now the lookup's query and `ZoneDatabase::LoadSingleTraderItem` is gone.
