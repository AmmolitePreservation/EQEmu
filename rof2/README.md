# One client: Rain of Fear 2

This branch removes every client but Rain of Fear 2 (the May 10 2013 build) from EQEmu. It starts from `main`'s tip 6fedcae84 and is the base the `guids` branch is rebased onto, which is where item GUIDs replace the last per-client item handle (`trader.item_sn`); player housing follows `guids`, and bots are rewritten after housing.

## What goes

- **The translators** for Titanium, Secrets of Faydwer, Seeds of Destruction, Underfoot and Rain of Fear (`common/patches/`: their patch, structs, opcode and limits files), the patch template and `makepatch`, the deprecated item field headers, and the five opcode files in `utils/patches/`.
- **The version enums.** `ClientVersion` is `Unknown` or `RoF2`; `MobVersion` keeps `RoF2` beside the NPC, merchant, mercenary, bot and pet versions. The UCS connection type is RoF2's combined chat and mail.
- **The client axis on content.** The client version is neither the era the server emulates (`Expansion:CurrentExpansion`, min and max expansion, content flags, a zone's `bypass_expansion_check`) nor the zone version (`version` rows per layout); with one client it carries no information. So `doors.client_version_mask` and `zone_points.client_version_mask` go with the check that tested the connected client's bit against them, the client bit itself (`ClientVersionBit()`, the bit enum, the bit conversions), the per-client-bit filters on `QueueClientsByTarget` and `QueueClientsByXTarget`, and the quest API's client version: `GetClientVersion` and `GetClientVersionBit` on a client, `GetClientVersionMask` on a door, and the Lua `ClientVersion` constants. Migration 9326 settles the rows the mask excluded (Database, below).
- **The limits tables** (`common/eq_limits.cpp`) keep one client row each. The expansion-driven inventory shape stays, as named constants: the bank has 16 slots before Prophecy of Ro, the power source slot comes with The Buried Sea, the ninth and tenth general slots with House of Thule; a GM keeps the slots but not the bank.
- **Every client version branch** collapses to RoF2's outcome, about 290 sites in zone, world, common, the chat server and the login server. Where RoF2 was the newer path, that path stays; where only older clients took a branch (Titanium consider colours, pre-SoF group and spell packets, pre-RoF guild rank conversions, the Berserker piercing skill swap), it goes.
- **The login server** listens for SoD-and-later and Larion clients; the Titanium listener, its opcode file `login_opcodes.conf` and the `titanium_port` and `titanium_opcodes` config keys go.
- **World** has one character creation check and one start zone lookup (the client sends the zone id, matched by zone, class, deity and race in `start_zones`), and the client version summary counts RoF2 clients.
- **The chat server** sends every message as RoF2 reads it; the say-link converters for the older clients go.
- **Mercenaries** no longer filter their types and templates by the client's version.
- **Rules:** `World:SupportedClients`, `World:SoFStartZoneID`, `World:TitaniumStartZoneID`, `Spells:FRProjectileItem_Titanium` and `Spells:FRProjectileItem_SOF` go; `World:StartZoneID` and `Spells:FRProjectileItem` take the place of the last three. `World:UseClientBasedExpansionSettings` goes too: the expansion mask a client is told it owns, and the inventory shape that follows from it, come from `World:ExpansionSettings` alone. `Character:SoDClientUseSoDHPManaEnd` keeps its name and now chooses the formula set for everyone.
- **Era tests that hid behind client tests** move to the era. The stat cap past level 70 (330 before Secrets of Faydwer, unbounded from it) follows `Expansion:CurrentExpansion`. A drink's intoxication, which upstream computed twice with the pre-SoD and SoD Alcohol Tolerance caps written in, is one formula on the cap the server serves at its maximum level.
- **Quest scripts** that tested the client version did so for the client's own capabilities. ProjectEQ's Halloween event scripts in Toxxulia (both layouts), Rivervale and Kithicor refused or warned Titanium clients, and two account-credit plugins printed the client's name; with one client the guards and the line go, and the plugin that named the client with them. The edits are a patch against ProjectEQ's quest repository (`rof2/peridotQuests.patch`), applied on peridotguids and to be applied to Peridot's checkout with the build.

## Many sessions per account

One account holds all of a player's characters and logs in many of them at once; what is exclusive is the character, not the account.

- **How many characters:** the rule `World:CharactersPerAccount` (default 12, RoF2's own creation limit). Character select lists that many, the client is told it may create up to it, and world refuses a creation past it. RoF2's character list scrolls past 12; the RoF2 creation limit constant and the encoder's cap at 12 go.
- **The login server** keeps every login session of an account (it no longer drops the older one) and routes world's answer to a play request by the session that asked: the request carries the session's number and world's answer brings it back.
- **World** hands a zone's acceptance to the session that asked (by its client list entry, not the account's first), a zoning client re-enters as its own session's character (not `account.charname`, the account's last), and a character entering the world drops that character wherever else it is, not every character of the account.
- **Zone** kicks and drops authorisation by character when the character enters again; the account's other characters stay.
- **The shared bank** is held by one session at a time, for one action: an item move into or out of it, a GM's `#nukeitem`, the zone-in removal of no-rent items and summoned bags. The hold is a MariaDB named lock per account (`<database>.shared_bank.<account>`) on the zone process's connection, which ends if the process dies, and a map within the process for sessions in the same zone (`SharedDatabase::TakeSharedBank`, `ReleaseSharedBank`, `IsSharedBankHolder`). Inside the hold the session's copy is compared with the account's `sharedbank` rows; when another session changed them, the copy is reloaded, the client's shared bank resent, and the action refused once ("Your shared bank was changed by another of your characters and has been refreshed"). An action while another session holds it is refused ("Another of your characters is using your shared bank"). A shared bank slot write by a session that does not hold it is refused at the database with an Error log. Take and release log at Info, refused actions at Warning.
- **Shared platinum** is read from the database at each coin move and withdrawn with a conditional update (only while the account holds at least that much), so two sessions cannot both withdraw it.

The rest of the account-wide state the sessions share (`account` row, account data buckets and flags) is the worklist's RC-12.

## What stays, and why

- **The era and zone version axes.** Content is still filtered by the server's current expansion and content flags when a zone loads, zoning still checks a zone's expansion, and a zone's layouts still live in `version` rows.
- **The `MobVersion` lookups**, since NPCs, mercenaries, bots and pets have inventory shapes of their own.

## Database

Migration 9326 (`2026_11_01_one_client_content.sql`, a content update) moves what ProjectEQ expressed on `client_version_mask` onto the era columns and drops the mask from `doors` and `zone_points`. ProjectEQ's data (the 2026-10-30 dump, which Peridot carries unmodified) has 6 doors and 17 zone points whose mask excluded RoF2, every one of them a pair with a twin at the same place and number:

- **Era pairs** become min and max expansion bounds taken from the revamp zone's own `expansion`. The Highpass lines from East Karana, High Keep (two) and Kithicor: classic Highpass (zone 5) through Prophecy of Ro (max 11), Highpass Hold (407) from The Serpent's Spine (min 12). The 2006 revamp lines, which ProjectEQ shipped with mask 0: commons and commonlands (Kithicor 3, Befallen 1, East Commons 2), innothule and innothuleb (Feerrott 1, Grobb 1, Guk 6), tox and toxxulia (Erudin 3, Paineel 1), misty and mistythicket (Rivervale 99, Runnyeye 2), split at 11 and 12; steamfont and steamfontmts (Ak'Anon 1000, Lesser Faydark 1), split at 13 and 14. Plane of Knowledge zone point 17 and the Corathus door `POKTELE500` both lead into Nektulos at two landings, the Lost Dungeons layout through Dragons of Norrath (max 9) and the Depths of Darkhollow layout from then on (min 10). 28 zone points and 2 doors change; none is deleted.
- **Client-file duplicates** go: the Plane of Knowledge door `POKRVPORT500` 36669, identical to 2040 but for the door index in the older clients' zone file, and the four Guild Hall mask-0 rows (`GUILD_DOOR`, two `OBJ_GUILD_DOOR`, `OBJ_SOLBSTATUE`), switched-off duplicates with no era meaning.

Migration 9327 (`2026_11_02_one_client_leftovers.sql`, a content update) drops `start_zones.player_choice`, Titanium's starting-city index, and re-keys the table on zone, race, class and deity, which Peridot's 420 rows allow without collision; and drops `bug_reports.client_version_id` and `client_version_name`, constant with one client. Both tables live in one database on Peridot, as the content entry assumes. Migration 9216's mercenary CREATE statements lose their `clientversion` columns, since that entry only runs where the tables are absent and nothing reads them; Peridot has no mercenary tables.

What the server sends now follows `Expansion:CurrentExpansion` alone: a Planes of Power server sends players from East Karana into classic Highpass and to the Lost Dungeons landing in Nektulos, a Serpent's Spine server into Highpass Hold. Whether the client can load what it is sent is the client package's business, not the database's: the RoF2 client has no zone 5 in its zone table (the client's log says "Unknown Zone" and fails to load a world file) and its Nektulos files are the Darkhollow layout. Peridot's rule rows for the removed and renamed rules needed nothing: world deletes rule rows it no longer knows and inserts missing defaults at boot. No client-axis column remains; the worklist (`rof2/worklist.md`) records each item with its evidence and tests.

## Deploying to Peridot

Peridot runs the same data peridotguids was tested on, so the steps are the ones peridotguids took:

1. Pull the branch and build it (`make` in the server's build directory; world, zone, ucs and loginserver).
2. Stop the server and run `./bin/world database:updates` from the server directory. It backs the database up, then applies 9326 and 9327 and sets `db_version` to 9327. Both are content entries; Peridot keeps content and player tables in one database, which they assume.
3. Apply `rof2/peridotQuests.patch` to the quests checkout (`git apply` from the quests directory; the checkout is ProjectEQ/projecteqquests with local changes, and the patch was taken against it). Without it the three Halloween NPCs refuse every player, since the scripts' client test fails for RoF2's new enum value.
4. Start the server. World deletes the rule rows the branch no longer knows (`World:SupportedClients`, `World:TitaniumStartZoneID`, `World:SoFStartZoneID`, `World:UseClientBasedExpansionSettings`, the two projectile item rules) and inserts the defaults of the renamed ones (`World:StartZoneID` -1, `Spells:FRProjectileItem` 80684). `World:ExpansionSettings` now governs the expansion mask on its own; Peridot already had the client-based rule off.
5. The login server's `login.json` loses nothing it needs: the Titanium port and opcode keys are ignored if left, and `login_opcodes.conf` can go.
6. The login server makes no accounts: `account.auto_create_accounts` is `false` in `login.json` (so in `loginserver/login_util/login.json` here; the server takes a missing key as `true`, so the key has to be there). Accounts are made by the Ammolite site through the login server's API, and a name typed at the login screen that has no account is refused rather than made into one. Peridot already runs with it off; its previous `login.json` is kept beside it as `login.json.beforeNoAutoCreate.20261112`.
7. Ammolite's check of logins, when a world switches to it: `site_login_check` in `login.json` holds `check_url` (Ammolite's `https://ammolite.unseencolor.com:1996/api/v1/loginServer/check`) and `token` (the token Ammolite issues for the world's login server, `POST /api/v1/admin/servers/<world>/loginServer`, shown once). Both or neither; without them the login server checks its own accounts as before. With them, a password login asks Ammolite about the name and password typed (the player's Ammolite name and password, or a login key from Ankh's Play) and the player's own address, logs in the login account Ammolite names, which Ammolite makes at the player's first good login, and reads no password hash of its own; an account's old password no longer works. The login server needs OpenSSL 1.1.1 or later for it and refuses to start with the section set otherwise. Peridot does not run it yet: its players' accounts first move into the accounts named after them.
8. Many sessions per account (README's "Many sessions per account"): set `World:CharactersPerAccount` to the characters one account may hold (peridotguids ran it at 100). Not before the worklist's RC-12: until then two sessions of one account share the `account` row unguarded. `World:EnforceCharacterLimitAtLogin` stays off, since it refuses an account already in the game.

Peridot's client package already carries what the era-bounded rows can send a Planes of Power player to, except classic Highpass, which the RoF2 client cannot load at all; East Karana's Highpass line sends players there at era 4 and the client stops at "Unable to load World". Either keep the era rule where it is and accept that line as dead, or move the client's Highpass files to the id the client knows; the database side is right either way.

## Ammolite's login check on peridotguids (2026-11-13)

The login server built with the check, `site_login_check` set and peridotguids linked to Ammolite as a test world: a stock RoF2 client typed an Ammolite name and password and reached character select on a new account Ammolite made there; an old account's own password was refused with the client's invalid username or password message. The check was then turned off, the test world removed from Ammolite and its account and token removed from peridotguids; claudelink's spare logged in with its old account again.

- The collapsed paths content had not exercised (RC-4), with two characters and chat logs: task objectives, updates and completion in the task window; guild invite, ranks, MOTD, chat, a permission refusal and removal; group and raid formation with member mana in both windows; pet commands and taunt persisting across a login; inspect; the Find window; reading a book; the parcel merchant's mail tab; death and respawn; extended-target slots.
